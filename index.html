Why YouTube Live streaming beats the competition

Legacy construction camera companies force you to log into a clunky proprietary portal and usually only snap 1 still photo every 15 minutes. Streaming live in 4K directly to YouTube changes the game:

  • Private for Staff & Investors, or Public for Marketing: You control who sees each camera. Keep streams private/unlisted strictly for internal staff, executives, and investors—or make them public to drive buyer excitement and SEO.
  • Watch on Any Phone, Computer, or Office Smart TV: Because it runs on YouTube, stakeholders can pull up live 4K feeds in one tap on their iPhone, iPad, or a 75" conference room Smart TV—zero portal logins or forgotten passwords.
  • We Set Up & Manage It — You Own Everything: We create, configure, and manage your YouTube channel and provide the embed codes for your website, while you retain 100% ownership of both.
  • Unlimited Viewers & Instant Website Embeds: Embed your live streams on any website with zero per-viewer bandwidth fees or seat limits.

All-weather durability + 24/7 jobsite security
